The Anchor · The Correlation Test — Black Friday Is the First Session Where Bitcoin Must Hold Its Thanksgiving Recovery Against Returning Equity-Market Correlation

Thursday’s Thanksgiving rebound raised a question that only Friday could answer: was the 4.7% gain genuine demand or a short-covering rally into a thin market? Friday’s answer, so far, is that the floor held. Bitcoin’s $61,579 close is above Thursday’s $60,734 — a small additional gain on a day when equity markets reopened with strong upward moves. The correlation returned on Friday, but as a tailwind: the Nasdaq’s 2.3% Black Friday gain lifted tech sentiment broadly, and BTC moved in that direction rather than fighting it. The more important test is what the correlation does on a neutral-to-negative equity day — a day when the Nasdaq is flat or down and crypto must find its own floor. That test has not happened this week. What is confirmed by Friday’s session: the $58,500 support from Wednesday held through the Thanksgiving low-liquidity environment and held again when equity markets came back. What is not resolved: seven consecutive ETF outflow sessions, with institutional holders continuing to reduce net exposure through the spot-price recovery. The divergence between spot-market buyers — who have been absorbing institutional selling for a week — and institutional ETF redemptions is the most important structural signal of the week. If ETF outflows continue next week without spot-price breakdown, the market is saying that retail demand is deep enough to absorb institutional exits without capitulating — a bullish structural read. If outflows continue and spot price rolls over, the floor thesis fails.
