The Anchor · 85.5% — What the Hike Odds Jump Means for BTC Positioning Into Monday and Tuesday

The 16-point jump in CME FedWatch's rate-hike probability — from 69.4% on Saturday to 85.5% on Sunday — is the largest single-weekend move in the probability since Kevin Warsh's Jackson Hole speech in late August began the sustained tightening expectations. At 85.5%, the market is no longer debating whether the Fed hikes on Tuesday; it is debating the statement language that determines whether Tuesday is a pause or a step in a continuation. For Bitcoin, which has held at approximately $77,243 through Sunday morning, the 85.5% probability means the 25bp hike is already in the price. The asset sold from $79,742 to $76,758 across Thursday and Friday on CPI and PPI data; it then briefly recovered to $79,890 on Saturday before retreating again as the hike probability resumed its climb. The mechanics of BTC at $77,243 on a Sunday when hike odds are at 85.5% are straightforward: the market has priced the hike; it cannot price it any more precisely until the decision is announced. What it is now pricing at the margin is the probability distribution of the statement. The Fed has two basic options: hike 25bp and signal that the September increase is potentially the last of this cycle (pause signal) — which would allow the market to begin pricing rate cuts in early 2027, a structurally positive development for non-yielding crypto in Q4 — or hike 25bp and indicate that additional increases remain on the table depending on data (continuation signal) — which would maintain the elevated opportunity cost of holding BTC and ETH through year-end. The market's current positioning at $77,243 reflects a rough balance between those scenarios. The CLARITY Act's failure tomorrow — still near-certain at Galaxy 10% and Polymarket 16% — removes US legislative upside but does not materially alter the regulatory status quo. The more interesting structural development is Nasdaq Ventures' $100 million investment in Kraken parent Payward for a tokenized equity framework: traditional financial infrastructure building toward a post-CLARITY world regardless of whether the legislation passes. That investment says the institutionalization of crypto infrastructure is proceeding on its own timeline even when the legislative calendar fails. By Wednesday morning, all three sequences — CLARITY, FOMC, EU GPAI — will have resolved. BTC's Q4 direction will be clearer than it has been at any point this year.
