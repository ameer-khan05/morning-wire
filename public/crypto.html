The Anchor · CPI 3.4% In-Line and What It Means for Crypto — Why "As Expected" Is Not Relief

The August CPI print at 3.4% annual — headline precisely in line, core at 2.4% in line — is the worst possible outcome for Bitcoin's short-term positioning that does not involve a negative surprise. A downside surprise (core below 2.2%) would have given the market a basis to reprice rate-hike probability lower and offered crypto a relief rally. An upside surprise (core above 2.6%) would have been bad news but expected. The in-line reading confirms that the inflationary environment the Fed has already decided to respond to is exactly as inflationary as forecasted — meaning the 65% rate-hike probability that has been compressing crypto this week is exactly as accurate as it was when it was calculated. There is no new information in "in-line" that changes the forward rate path. The 10-year Treasury at 4.89% has already priced the September 16 hike; the 65% CME FedWatch probability has already priced it; Bitcoin's two-day decline from $79,742 through $77,941 to $76,758 has already priced a significant fraction of it. What "in-line" removes is the scenario in which Thursday's PPI (which was hot enough to add to the hike case) was the stronger inflation signal and Friday's CPI was a moderating factor. That moderating-CPI scenario does not exist today. Both PPI and CPI have now confirmed the same underlying inflationary environment. The FOMC's decision on Tuesday is effectively set by the accumulated data: 3.4% headline CPI, a hot PPI, 162,000 August nonfarm payrolls, and $107 Brent crude. The question for Tuesday is not whether to hike but by how much — the market is pricing 25 basis points — and whether the statement signals pause or continuation. The CLARITY Act on Monday adds legislative uncertainty to a macro environment that is already fully loaded. At Galaxy's 30% and Polymarket's 17%, the market is pricing CLARITY failure without treating it as a foregone conclusion. A Monday CLARITY failure would not move BTC meaningfully on its own — the FOMC is the dominant signal — but it removes the upside optionality that a CLARITY passage would have created. Crypto enters the Sep 15-16 sequence with maximum rate uncertainty, confirmed inflationary data, and no legislative upside in the base case. $76,758 is Bitcoin's response.
