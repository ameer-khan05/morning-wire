Thursday’s crypto session is the chip-fear contagion spreading to digital assets: Wednesday’s election hedge unwind is being partially re-wound, with the same Democratic Congress outcome that removed political risk on Wednesday now introducing regulatory risk on Thursday, as investors ask what a Democratic majority will do with crypto oversight in January 2027.

The Anchor · BTC Below $79K — Wednesday’s Hedge Unwind Meets Thursday’s Re-Hedge

Wednesday’s election hedge unwind sent BTC from $77,000 to $80,300. Thursday’s re-hedge has sent it back below $79,000. The same election result is being re-priced in the opposite direction in less than 24 hours — not incoherence, but the market processing two different risk implications of the same event in sequence. Wednesday’s read: Democratic sweep removes legislative uncertainty, Digital Assets bill path clears. Thursday’s read: a Democratic Congress may pursue more aggressive crypto regulation than the alternative. Both reads are rational; the resolution depends on what Democratic leadership signals about crypto regulation in the post-election period — a signal that has not yet arrived. The ETH derivatives longs rising $435 million to $868 million are the sophisticated-money bet that Thursday’s selloff is a temporary re-hedge, not a new directional move.

Russia’s institutional investor base, redirected toward domestic markets after Western financial exclusion since 2022, represents a new pool of compliant capital that has never had a regulated crypto on-ramp at this scale. The December 1 structural demand signal matters more than the session price it launches at.
