The Anchor · $634 Million in One Hour — How Tuesday’s Short Squeeze Doubled Monday’s Record and What It Means for the Post-FOMC Rally

Monday’s $262 million in short liquidations in one hour at $84,000 was the largest single-hour Bitcoin short cascade since March. Tuesday’s $634 million in one hour is 2.4 times larger. The scale difference is explained by what happened overnight: short sellers who opened new positions after Monday’s squeeze, betting that Bitcoin would not sustain above $84,000 and would retrace to $82,000–$83,000 overnight, encountered a market on Tuesday morning that instead opened above $86,500. Their short positions, established at prices 2–3% below the open, were immediately underwater and forced into liquidation as price moved up rather than down. The cascading effect — shorts forced to buy at successively higher prices as liquidations triggered market orders — compressed through three resistance levels in a single session: $85,000, $86,000, and $87,000. The mechanics of Tuesday’s squeeze tell a specific story about the post-FOMC recovery’s character. Monday’s squeeze cleared short interest that had accumulated below $84,000 — positions opened in anticipation that the FOMC-week sell would resume. Tuesday’s squeeze cleared short interest that was re-opened after Monday’s squeeze — positions opened by traders who believed the Monday move was excessive and would reverse. The fact that both sets of shorts were liquidated in two consecutive sessions means the post-FOMC recovery has now cleared two rounds of short-side capital: the original bears and the second-wave contrarians. After two consecutive record-scale short squeezes, the short interest remaining in Bitcoin is structurally reduced. The next significant directional move will be determined by organic demand rather than short-covering amplification — which means it will be both slower and more durable. BTC at $87,250 entering Wednesday’s Trump-Xi summit is the clearest available risk-on reading of pre-summit positioning. A risk asset that has just cleared $634 million in shorts in one hour is not positioned for a defensive summit outcome. The market is pricing a constructive bilateral — and if Trump and Xi produce a narrow AI safety accord or any tariff relief, the organic demand that was buying BTC through $87,000 will have its fundamental thesis validated in the same 24 hours that the technical structure was cleared. The asymmetric risk heading into Wednesday is the familiar one: if the summit produces something, BTC continues; if it disappoints, the next support is the cleared short interest’s departure price — around $84,000, which has now been established as a structurally significant level twice in two days.
