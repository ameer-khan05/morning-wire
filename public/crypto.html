Bitcoin at $64,972 and Ethereum at $1,916 on Saturday morning reflect normal weekend consolidation after Friday's jobs-driven rally — when crypto assets rose alongside equities as July's stunning -23,000 nonfarm payrolls figure (versus the +80,000 consensus) collapsed September rate-hike probability from 58% to 42%, gold surged 2.37%, and Treasury 10-year yields fell 7 basis points to 4.64%; for crypto, the jobs shock's mechanism is the standard rate-policy transmission: lower probability of rate hikes reduces the opportunity cost of holding BTC and ETH versus cash, the dollar weakens on falling rate expectations, and risk appetite improves across all asset classes — the same three-part process that has made each dovish macro development a BTC and ETH support event throughout 2026. The CLARITY Act's September 15-16 floor vote commitment from Majority Leader Thune remains the crypto legislative calendar's key near-term event, and the September FOMC — where rate hike probability has now fallen to 42% — converges in the same month as CLARITY, making September simultaneously the most important regulatory and monetary-policy period for digital assets since January 2024's spot ETF approval.

The rate-policy context deserves nuance: 42% hike probability means a rate hike is now the minority view for September — but it also means a cut is not yet the majority view, with roughly 58% of market pricing on hold or cut territory. The jobs shock was large enough to remove hike risk; whether the data accumulation between now and September 16 confirms a cut or a hold will determine the second half of the macro tailwind that crypto's most bullish September scenario requires. In the meantime, the structural supply story for ETH — 1 million-plus coins accumulated by a public company since early 2026, reducing liquid supply — and the GENIUS stablecoin bill's independent advancement both provide demand floor mechanics that operate regardless of the rate-policy outcome.

Gold's 2.37% surge to $4,401 on Friday is the clearest single expression of this mechanism — a large, liquid safe-haven asset rising sharply as rates fell. BTC and ETH move directionally with gold in this framework, just with higher volatility. September FOMC convergence with CLARITY: for the first time, the most favorable crypto macro scenario (rate hold or cut) and the most favorable crypto regulatory scenario (CLARITY passage) are scheduled for the same calendar month. The probability of both happening is lower than either alone, but the convergence creates a specific catalyst density that September lacked in any previous planning horizon. The jobs shock changed the rate-policy narrative more completely than most data points do: going from 58% hike probability to 42% in a single day is a meaningful repricing, not a marginal adjustment. Crypto markets absorbed it correctly — prices rose on Friday, then gave back a fraction on Saturday as markets processed the nuance that 42% hike probability is not the same as confirmed cut. The key data points between now and September 15 are: August inflation data (CPI, PCE) — which direction does the rate-hike probability move as disinflation either continues or stalls? The jobs data was dovish; if inflation data is also dovish, the September cut probability rises and crypto's macro tailwind strengthens further. If inflation data is mixed, 42% hike probability persists and September is a hold with binary CLARITY upside. The GENIUS stablecoin bill's independent advancement remains crypto's regulatory floor in both scenarios.

XRP's test of the $1 support level is the secondary price development worth tracking this weekend: the $1 level has marked a significant technical threshold since early 2026, and whether XRP holds it on a Saturday — when weekend liquidity is thinner and moves can be exaggerated — provides signal about the underlying demand structure at current prices. CLARITY Act: September 15-16 Floor Vote Committed (Thune) — September Hike Odds 42%, Cut More Likely — Both September Catalysts Favorable for First Time · Unresolved: Yield Rights, Ethics, Law Enforcement · August Recess Negotiating Window · Enactment Still 2027 At Earliest Sept 16 CLARITY: Sept 15-16 floor vote (Thune commitment) · procedure: cloture by Mon Sept 15 → vote Sept 15-16 · unresolved: yield rights, ethics, law enforcement data · August recess = negotiating window · September FOMC: hike 42% (minority view) → September = first month with both regulatory + monetary catalysts favorable · enactment: 2027 at earliest even if passes

The CLARITY Act's September 15-16 floor vote commitment from Majority Leader Thune is unchanged — the bill has a defined procedural path, the recess provides negotiating time for the unresolved issues, and the September FOMC now has rate-hike probability at 42% (minority view), meaning September is simultaneously the most favorable regulatory and monetary-policy month for crypto that has existed in 2026; the convergence of a CLARITY vote and a Fed meeting where hikes are no longer the majority view creates a specific catalyst density that markets have not had to price simultaneously before. The practical implication is that September's outcome set has three scenarios: (1) CLARITY passes + rate hold or cut = maximum positive catalyst, (2) CLARITY fails + rate hold or cut = positive macro, negative regulation, (3) CLARITY passes + rate hike = positive regulation, negative macro. The first scenario is now less improbable than it was before the jobs report moved hike odds to 42%.

The three unresolved CLARITY issues — stablecoin yield rights, ethics rules for public officials holding crypto, and law enforcement data access provisions — are all fundamentally political negotiations rather than technical drafting questions. Yield rights: the banking lobby objects because regulated interest-bearing stablecoins compete with bank deposit products; the resolution requires either removing yield rights from the bill or convincing the banking lobby that yield-bearing stablecoins serve a distinct market. Ethics rules: the Trump WLFI position creates a conflict that bipartisan ethics negotiators need to resolve in a way that neither partisan side can openly oppose. Law enforcement data access: crypto-privacy advocates and law enforcement agencies have directly opposing interests. All three have precedents from previous legislative compromises; whether the August recess produces the political will to close them is the variable that determines whether Thune's September 15-16 commitment holds.

ETH's structural supply story is unchanged from Thursday's analysis — a public company has accumulated over 1 million ETH on its balance sheet since early 2026, removing approximately 6-10% of circulating exchange float from the liquid market in a manner that structural analysis treats as equivalent to demand, because balance-sheet ETH does not circulate on exchanges and is not subject to the forced-selling pressure that leveraged positions create — and the GENIUS stablecoin bill's continued independent advancement through Congress provides a regulatory demand floor for ETH as the settlement layer for the $317 billion stablecoin market regardless of what happens to CLARITY in September. The two mechanisms — supply compression and regulated settlement demand — operate independently of each other and independently of the rate-policy macro story, providing ETH with three separate support structures that are not correlated.

XRP's test of $1 support on Saturday morning carries specific significance for the CLARITY outcome scenario: the SEC/CFTC jurisdictional question that determines whether XRP is a security (SEC enforcement) or a commodity (CFTC framework) is explicitly addressed by CLARITY's market-structure provisions, making the September floor vote a direct potential catalyst for XRP's regulatory classification pathway. Courts have partially resolved the question through litigation, but statutory CLARITY passage would provide a definitive framework. If $1 holds through the weekend and into next week's thin pre-recess trading, the technical structure for a CLARITY-driven move higher stays intact; if $1 breaks, the base for that scenario shifts lower and the risk-reward of holding XRP through September's uncertainty changes.

Quick hits

  • The relationship between the July jobs report and BTC's price deserves an explicit mechanism map for readers tracking crypto through a macro lens. July NFP −23,000 → (1) September rate-hike probability falls from 58% to 42% → (2) dollar weakens relative to expected future value → (3) gold and commodities priced in dollars rise (gold +2.37%) → (4) risk appetite improves broadly as the "higher for longer" scenario becomes less probable → (5) BTC and ETH, which are priced in dollars and positively correlated with broad risk appetite, rise on Friday. The ▼0.26% and ▼0.68% Saturday pullbacks reflect the same mechanism running slightly in reverse as weekend liquidity reduces the immediate pressure, not the disappearance of the underlying rate-policy signal. The signal is still in place: 42% hike probability persists until new data changes it. August CPI (released before the September 16 FOMC) will be the next major variable to move the 42% number up or down.
  • Thune's September 15-16 commitment is worth holding against a simple political-economy test: does Thune have a stronger incentive to honor the commitment or break it? The case for honoring: a public floor-vote commitment carries more political cost to break than a missed implicit deadline; Thune needs crypto industry and industry-adjacent Republican donor support for the 2026 midterm cycle; the September recess gives negotiators the time they did not have in August. The case for breaking: if the yield-rights, ethics, and law enforcement issues genuinely cannot be resolved during the recess, Thune faces the same Senate scheduling calendar competition that prevented August action; if foreign-policy developments (Iran, Ukraine, Taiwan) consume Senate floor time in September, CLARITY can be displaced again without a procedural commitment being formally broken. The honest probability assessment for September passage is in the 30-45% range — meaningfully higher than "dead," meaningfully lower than "certain." A market pricing CLARITY passage at a specific September date is assigning probability where genuine uncertainty exists.
