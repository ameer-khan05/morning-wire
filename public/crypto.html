Quick hits

  • The GENIUS Act — the stablecoin legislation signed in July 2025 — established the federal regulatory framework for payment stablecoins that CLARITY's passage is designed to complement for the broader digital asset market structure. Full GENIUS Act implementation arrives January 2027, meaning the stablecoin framework will be operational regardless of CLARITY's outcome. The asymmetry between a settled stablecoin framework (GENIUS, implemented) and an uncertain market structure framework (CLARITY, 20% passage odds) is the current state of US crypto legislation — the most commercially critical layer (stablecoin payments) has regulatory clarity, while the broader asset classification layer (what is a security vs. a commodity) remains contested. Institutional crypto adoption is advancing faster in stablecoin payments infrastructure than in spot trading assets, partly because the regulatory basis for each is so different.
  • BTC's position below its cost-basis estimate for average market participants — approximately $65,000–$67,000 in most on-chain cost models — has now persisted for several weeks. The historical pattern for extended below-cost-basis periods is either capitulation (a sharp move lower before recovery) or base formation (gradual accumulation by long-term holders at lower prices before a catalyst-driven recovery). The White House summit and September dual catalyst are exactly the type of regulatory-macro event that has historically driven crypto out of base-forming periods. Whether the summit produces the catalyst quality the technical setup needs will determine which historical pattern this period most resembles when viewed in retrospect.
