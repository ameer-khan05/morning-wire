The American Heart Association’s Scientific Sessions 2026 opened in Chicago with two landmark negative results: the ZEUS trial of ziltivekimab missed its primary endpoint of reducing major adverse cardiovascular events despite successfully lowering hs-CRP inflammation markers; and the Lp(a) HORIZON trial of pelacarsen also missed its primary endpoint despite reducing lipoprotein(a) by over 60% — a double setback for two emerging drug pipelines; a third study found heart scans recommended fewer statin prescriptions but produced the same cardiovascular outcomes; and University of Cologne researchers identified leucine’s role in stabilizing mitochondrial proteins to boost energy production.

Two trials hit their biomarker targets, neither hit their clinical goal — cardiology’s most repeated lesson is repeated again at AHA’s most watched meeting of the year.
