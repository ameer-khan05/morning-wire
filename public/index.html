By the Numbers
65%
LDL cholesterol reduction in clinical trials of Lipfendra (enlicitide), the first oral PCSK9 inhibitor approved by the FDA. PCSK9 inhibitors were previously available only as biweekly injections at $5,000–$7,000 annually; enlicitide is a once-daily pill at $315 per month. The drug opens the most effective class of LDL treatment — injectable-class efficacy — to millions of statin patients who could not access it through an injectable regimen. Taken on an empty stomach with water or black coffee.
