The combination of surging agentic adoption and lagging governance in the Deloitte data defines the central enterprise AI risk of 2026: autonomous agents are being deployed faster than the organizational frameworks to oversee them are being built.

Iran retaliated by striking U.S. military forces based in Jordan and hitting the Al Minhad Air Base in the UAE, the most significant escalation of the 62-day Hormuz crisis. President Trump separately confirmed that the United States has struck a deal with Venezuela giving the U.S. control over more than 65 billion barrels of Venezuelan oil, which he said would more than double American reserves and lower gasoline prices.

The International Criminal Court issued arrest warrants for both Vladimir Putin and Benjamin Netanyahu — a geopolitically explosive development that threatens the ICC's relationships with major Western governments that back Israel, while simultaneously cementing Russia's international pariah status. Russian strikes killed approximately 40 people in Ukraine, continuing the sustained aerial campaign against Ukrainian territory. Israeli settlers attacked a Palestinian home in Qusra in the occupied West Bank; Prime Minister Netanyahu condemned the violence and called the attackers lawbreakers.

CAR T-cell therapy dramatically reduced disease activity in 6 people with severe rheumatoid arthritis that had resisted other treatments, with 3 of the 6 achieving medication-free remission — a result that extends the cancer-proven cell therapy into autoimmune disease territory. Adults aged 40 and older who used a HEPA air purifier at home for one month performed approximately 12% faster on executive function tests, suggesting that cleaner indoor air may offer meaningful cognitive protection in mid-life and beyond. Researchers identified a brain-based "brake" that can shut down chronic nerve pain in mice by calming an overactive pain circuit, pointing toward a potential new class of chronic pain treatments.

The CAR T-cell RA result is the most clinically significant of Monday's science stories: rheumatoid arthritis affects approximately 18 million people globally, and medication-free remission — the outcome in half of the small trial — is rare even with the best available biologics. Three of six is too small a trial for definitive conclusions, but the direction of effect is striking enough to accelerate the next-stage trials.
