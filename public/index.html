Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh delivered a hawkish first Jackson Hole keynote Friday, declaring inflation "still too high" and the Fed has "more work to do" — sending September rate-hike odds surging to 55.7% on CME FedWatch and triggering a broad equity selloff that reversed much of Thursday's Nvidia-led gain, with the S&P 500 falling 0.30% to 7,715.47, the Nasdaq falling 0.50% to 26,411.07, and the 10-year Treasury yield rising to approximately 4.84% from Thursday's 4.77%. OpenAI and Broadcom simultaneously published detailed benchmarks for the Jalapeño inference chip — 13.4 PFLOPs at 700W on TSMC N3P, 1.5–1.9× better energy efficiency than Nvidia on GPT-OSS, DeepSeek R1, and Kimi K2.5 — confirming the first hyperscaler-class custom silicon to cross the inference efficiency threshold. Bitcoin briefly touched $81,000 — its highest since May — before Warsh's speech pulled it back to approximately $79,300, with 18 days remaining until the September 15 CLARITY Act cloture vote; the CLARITY structural bid held at $79,300 after the rate-cut tailwind was explicitly removed. On the world front, Iran's Foreign Minister said diplomacy "isn't impossible" while setting conditions for Hormuz reopening — lifting sanctions, US military withdrawal, war reparations, frozen assets — that CNN confirmed Washington is not negotiating. NASA cleared the Roman Space Telescope for its August 30 SpaceX Falcon Heavy launch, and Oxford researchers found the Shingrix shingles vaccine linked to 24% lower dementia risk in more than 500,000 people over four years.

OpenAI and Broadcom published full benchmarks for the Jalapeño inference chip Friday — 13.4 PFLOPs of MXFP4 compute at 700W on TSMC N3P, with 1.5 to 1.9 times better energy efficiency than Nvidia across the three LLM workloads OpenAI runs at scale — confirming the first hyperscaler-equivalent custom silicon to cross the inference-efficiency threshold that makes Nvidia substitutable for inference workloads. The chip deploys in OpenAI's production infrastructure by year-end; second and third silicon generations are already in development. Simultaneously, Fed Chair Warsh's hawkish Jackson Hole keynote sent rate-hike odds to 55.7% and the Nasdaq fell 0.50%, partially unwinding Thursday's Nvidia-fueled gain — the two stories, Jalapeño's positive capability signal and Warsh's negative rate-environment signal, defining Friday's tech investment backdrop.

Nvidia's contract server manufacturers confirmed to Microsoft, Google, and Oracle that AI server prices will rise more than 15% in early 2027 as Blackwell Ultra and Rubin advanced packaging costs flow through to buyers. A federal judge separately ruled the Pentagon cannot blacklist Anthropic for enforcing AI safety lines — a ruling that affirms AI companies' ability to decline certain use cases on safety grounds without government retaliation. AWS announced the September 30 shutdown of Mechanical Turk, the 21-year-old crowdsourced task platform, a quiet milestone marking AI models' displacement of the human annotation workforce that originally trained them.

Iran's Foreign Minister said Friday that diplomatic negotiations "aren't impossible," the softest diplomatic signal from Tehran since the Hormuz crisis began 59 days ago — while simultaneously laying out conditions for reopening the Strait that require lifting all US sanctions, withdrawing American military forces from the region, paying war reparations, and releasing frozen assets. CNN confirmed the US says it is "not talking to Iran," a direct contradiction of Tehran's diplomatic framing. Mine-clearing operations have not begun, shipping remains at 5 commodity vessels per day versus the pre-crisis average of 15, and Brent crude held at approximately $87.87 as markets balanced Iran's rhetorical softening against the absence of any operational progress. CIA Director Ratcliffe traveled to Moscow this week to privately urge Russian leadership to accept a negotiated settlement while its military and economic position remains stronger than it will be if the war continues.

Taiwan's opposition-controlled legislature rejected the government's $6.6 billion drone defense plan and passed its own $7.6 billion spending bill — $1 billion more than the government proposed — signaling bipartisan urgency on drone procurement while contesting who controls the spending. The cross-strait dynamic has shifted from whether to arm with drones to how much and under whose authority.

NASA's Roman Space Telescope received "Go" for its August 30 launch on a SpaceX Falcon Heavy from Kennedy Space Center — a $3.93 billion observatory whose 2.96-billion-pixel, 300-megapixel Wide Field Instrument will survey the sky 100 times faster than Hubble, enabling the first precision measurement of dark energy's equation of state, dark matter galaxy formation maps, and the discovery of thousands of new exoplanets. Its coronagraph will directly image giant planets orbiting nearby stars, combining cosmological survey and exoplanet direct-imaging capability in a single observatory. An Oxford-led study of 500,000+ adults found that Shingrix shingles vaccine recipients were 24% less likely to be diagnosed with dementia over four years — roughly one case prevented per 17 vaccinated — with additional benefits against stroke and vascular dementia. Laboratory experiments published Friday found that vanillin, the vanilla flavoring in popular e-cigarettes, disrupts the earliest steps of human development in cell experiments.

The Shingrix dementia finding, if confirmed in randomized trials, would represent the most significant development in dementia prevention since the disease became the leading cause of death globally — an immediately deployable prevention strategy using a vaccine already recommended for adults over 50. Roman's Sunday launch marks the beginning of the most ambitious cosmological survey in history.
