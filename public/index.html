Oman's Foreign Ministry characterized the talks as "positive and constructive" at both technical and political levels, while US Secretary of State Marco Rubio responded to the Araghchi statement by saying there has been "progress made, but not finality" — maintaining the US position that a deal has not yet been completed.

The asymmetry between Iran's "on the verge" framing and the US "not finality" position is not necessarily a contradiction: the two governments may be describing the same state of negotiations from different political vantage points, with Iran's public optimism serving a domestic audience that needs to see progress while US caution manages domestic expectations about concessions. With 2 days until the August 12 total solar eclipse, weather forecasts have shifted the best ground-observation prospects to Spain — Palencia, Valladolid, and Mallorca are all looking clear — while Iceland is forecast 70-80% cloudy based on historical patterns for mid-August, inverting Saturday's outlook and redirecting late-deciding eclipse observers to the path's southern end; NASA's WB-57, flying the totality path at 460 mph with four cameras in nine wavelengths, is positioned above cloud interference and will have approximately 3 minutes of totality airborne, significantly more than Iceland's 2-minute ground maximum. Tuesday is also August CPI morning (8:30am ET), a dual-event that makes the day's first financial print arrive before Iceland's totality begins at 09:27 UTC.

Separately this week: a wearable patch in a 28-participant study shortened the time to REM sleep by 43 minutes and extended REM duration by 16 minutes without medication or surgery — a non-pharmacological sleep intervention result with broad potential. Researchers also found that a protein called C3, produced inside tumors, blocks the immune-suppressing cells that limit immunotherapy effectiveness — providing a potential mechanism for making immunotherapy work for patients who currently don't respond.
