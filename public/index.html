Colorado became the first US state to regulate AI chatbots specifically to protect minors, establishing age verification and content restrictions for platforms with underage users — a narrower, use-case-specific regulatory approach than California's broad liability and safety-testing framework. Applied Materials fell 2.48% after reporting solid quarterly earnings that Wall Street characterized as failing to impress, consistent with Cisco's dynamic and suggesting that AI-adjacent earnings beats are no longer sufficient without margin expansion in August 2026.

The International Energy Agency warned Thursday that global oil stockpiles are dwindling rapidly as traffic through the Strait of Hormuz remains low, and that a reopening has become "more pressing" — shifting the crisis's urgency from the diplomatic timeline to the economic one. Iran and Oman have signaled they are close to a deal, but Iranian officials insist the strait will not fully reopen until the US agrees to end the war, withdraw troops, and meet other long-sought concessions. Oman separately warned that ongoing attacks on shipping could derail progress on the private technical framework that has been the crisis's most productive diplomatic channel.

The US Joint Chiefs chairman privately communicated to the White House this week that the military needs an "off-ramp" from the Iran conflict, characterizing escalation options as likely to backfire — the first reported instance of senior military leadership advising against further escalation in the 45-day crisis. The UNSC vote on August 17 remains 3 days away with three unresolved text problems from the Vela Nova enforcement action, Iran's parliamentary permanent ban on Israeli cargo, and the Trump compensation demand. Brent at $87.07 reflects the IEA demand-destruction signal beginning to weigh against the enforcement-demonstration floor.

Amazon Tipping Point by 2040s — Nature: 22-28% Deforestation + 1.5°C = Irreversible Savanna Shift · 17-18% Already Deforested · Astrocytes Rebuild Damaged Neural Tissue: UZH Overturns Irreplaceability Assumption · GLP-1 AUD: Off-Label Prescribing Accelerates Post-CU Anschutz 2040s Amazon: Nature → 22-28% deforestation + 1.5°C → tipping point by 2040s → 70%+ of Amazon Basin affected · 17-18% already deforested · 2026 El Niño data accelerated timeline · Astrocytes (UZH): "regenerative" astrocytes rebuild damaged neural tissue in adult brains → transport nuclei to damaged areas · GLP-1 AUD: off-label prescribing conversation accelerating after CU Anschutz AUD trial

A new paper published in Nature determined that the Amazon rainforest could reach an irreversible tipping point as early as the 2040s if deforestation reaches 22-28% combined with global warming of 1.5-1.9°C — a threshold that current trajectories are tracking toward, given that 17-18% of the Amazon has already been deforested and temperatures are projected to hit 1.5°C above preindustrial levels annually as early as 2030. Crossing the threshold would transform more than 70% of the Amazon Basin from tropical rainforest to drier savanna within decades, releasing vast stored carbon and eliminating a critical sink at the moment climate models most depend on it. The research drew on 2026 El Niño drought and fire data that showed Amazon stress more severe than prior models predicted.

Researchers at the University of Zurich identified a specialized population of "regenerative" astrocytes in the adult brain — support cells that, when neighboring astrocytes are lost through injury or autoimmune disease, step in by transporting newly formed nuclei into damaged areas to rebuild lost cellular networks. The finding overturns the assumption that astrocytes in the adult brain are irreplaceable once lost, opening paths toward therapies for traumatic brain injury, multiple sclerosis, and neuromyelitis optica. GLP-1 off-label prescribing for alcohol use disorder is accelerating in addiction medicine following the CU Anschutz trial findings published this week.
