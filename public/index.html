Equity markets closed Friday sharply lower as AI “bubble” concerns dragged the Nasdaq down 2.1% to 26,140 — extending a weekly loss of 4.5%, the sharpest decline for the index since spring — while the S&P 500 fell 1.1% to 7,659 and the Dow shed only 0.3% to 52,002 as investors rotated from growth into defensives; all of this in the same week Google, Meta, and Microsoft simultaneously shipped major new AI model releases including Gemini 4 Argon, Muse Wonder, and MAI-Base-2, a striking disconnect between an accelerating AI product frontier and deteriorating AI equity sentiment. Brent crude surged 3.7% to $109.86 and WTI jumped 2.6% to $99.02, lifted by reports that Iranian attacks on shipping in and around the Strait of Hormuz have struck more than two dozen vessels since October began, pushing the geopolitical risk premium toward a new ceiling. President Trump waived key Russia-related sanctions and tariffs hours before they were set to take effect under legislation he signed last month, triggering a public confrontation with Zelensky that put US military intelligence support for Ukraine at risk; Brazil’s Lula fell behind his right-wing challenger ahead of the October 25 second-round runoff; and France erupted in nationwide demonstrations on Saturday over rising consumer prices and cuts to social security. The 2026 State of the Climate Report found seven of sixteen planetary vital signs at record extremes, the James Webb Space Telescope revealed a galaxy cluster with dark matter density that challenges standard models of cosmic structure formation, and optogenetic gene therapy was shown to partly restore functional sight in blind patients.
