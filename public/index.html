Russia and Ukraine concluded the Abu Dhabi talks’ second and final day with a major prisoner-of-war exchange but no ceasefire framework, as US mediators cited a “lack of trust” and Russia struck six electricity substations in Ukraine overnight; Iran’s security chief threatened to strike US military bases following Secretary Rubio’s assertion that large-scale combat operations against Iran were no longer necessary, while NATO scrambled jets after Ukrainian drones strayed into Baltic states’ airspace. A Lancet Commission validation study found that a BMI-alternative obesity diagnostic framework would flip the diagnoses of millions — raising measured prevalence 3.3–7.4% and separating “clinical” from “preclinical” obesity more accurately — and Israel’s Weizmann Institute published two Nature papers showing DNA-programmed materials that move, change shape, and beat in rhythm without any external control signal.

By the Numbers
$1.33T
Fully diluted valuation target for Anthropic’s initial public offering launched Wednesday on Nasdaq under the ticker “ANT” at $70–$78 per share. At the midpoint, the IPO raises $61 billion and prices Anthropic at $1.33 trillion — the highest valuation of any company to debut on a US exchange. At the top of the range ($78/share) it rises to $1.5 trillion. Seven strategic partners including Google and Nvidia have agreed to purchase $26 billion in non-voting stock at the IPO price. The offering would surpass Saudi Aramco’s $29.4 billion record IPO (2019) as the largest fundraise in US stock market history. Trading is expected to begin next week.

A Lancet Commission validation study published Wednesday found that a BMI-alternative obesity diagnostic framework — incorporating waist circumference, waist-to-height ratio, and organ function alongside body mass index — would flip the diagnoses of millions and raise measured prevalence 3.3–7.4%, while identifying a “clinical obesity” group at substantially higher mortality risk than BMI alone can detect; scientists at Israel’s Weizmann Institute published two Nature papers showing that DNA-programmed materials embedded with motor proteins can move, change shape, and beat in rhythm without any external control signal, laying groundwork for genetically programmable soft robots and in-body therapeutic materials; the Congo Ebola outbreak surpassed 11,000 confirmed cases — one of the largest on record — while Kenya discharged its final Ebola patient and began the 42-day countdown to an Ebola-free declaration.

The Lancet obesity study’s key finding: the BMI-alternative framework separates people whose excess body fat is already impairing organ function (clinical obesity) from those biologically at risk but not yet symptomatic (preclinical obesity) — two groups that BMI alone cannot distinguish but that require fundamentally different treatment urgency.
