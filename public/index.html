Amazon announced it will shut down Mechanical Turk on September 30, 2026, ending the 21-year-old crowdsourced human intelligence platform that Jeff Bezos once called "artificial artificial intelligence." Amazon is simultaneously shutting SageMaker Ground Truth, its associated data labeling service. The cause of death is the AI models the platform spent two decades training: modern systems handle annotation, classification, and transcription at lower cost than human crowdsourcing, and Scale AI, Mercor, and Prolific have built more sophisticated pipelines for the advanced training data that today's frontier models require.

OpenAI announced Private Safety Processing, a monitoring tool that watches for enterprise abuse while retaining zero customer data — directly competing with Anthropic on privacy for business users, where OpenAI is already outpacing its rival through Q3. The EU AI Act gained new enforcement teeth: regulators can now inspect AI model providers, restrict EU market access, and fine companies up to €15 million or 3% of annual turnover.

NASA's Roman Space Telescope lifted off at 7:26 a.m. EDT Sunday from Launch Complex 39A at Kennedy Space Center aboard a SpaceX Falcon Heavy, separating from the rocket 31 minutes later with solar panels beginning to deploy. A post-launch press conference is underway. Roman is now in space, beginning a six-month journey to the L2 point 1 million miles from Earth — where it will conduct the first precision measurement of dark energy's equation of state, map dark matter through gravitational lensing, and discover thousands of exoplanets with its 2.96-billion-pixel camera covering a sky field 100 times wider than Hubble's.

Sunday's successful launch marks the completion of a 20-year development program. Data from Roman's wide-field infrared surveys will begin arriving within months of today's launch. The Shingrix dementia study continues to prompt formal calls for randomized controlled trials from multiple research groups, who say the observational evidence from 500,000+ adults has crossed the threshold that justifies the investment.
