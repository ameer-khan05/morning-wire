BlackRock's IBIT alone absorbed $454 million of that total, marking institutional confidence in BTC at current levels as the rate-hold scenario becomes more plausible on Waller's conditional framing.

The August jobs report at 162,000 — nearly triple the 55,000 consensus — created the initial rate-hike pressure that sent both assets briefly lower before Waller's comments reframed the moment: a strong economy is consistent with a rate hold if inflation is not accelerating, and Waller signaled the CPI on September 10 will be the arbiter. The CLARITY Act's September 15 cloture vote is now 11 days away; the September calendar's three-event convergence — CPI, CLARITY, FOMC — remains the context for every crypto price move between now and the 16th.

US equity markets were mixed Friday after the August payrolls report delivered 162,000 new jobs — nearly three times the 55,000 consensus estimate — with the unemployment rate holding unchanged at 4.1%, prompting an initial sell-off in both stocks and bonds before Federal Reserve Governor Chris Waller's comment that he would wait for next week's CPI data before deciding on rates moderated the decline. The S&P 500 edged down 0.05% to 7,644.98, the Nasdaq gained 0.09% to 26,022.08, the Dow fell 0.16% to 52,838.82, and the Russell 2000 dropped 1.40% to 2,912.00, with small caps bearing the sharpest rate-hike pressure as floating-rate-exposed companies priced the possibility of a September hike most directly.

The 10-year Treasury yield rose to 4.79% on the jobs beat, with the September rate-hike probability dropping from 65–70% pre-Waller back to approximately 50% after his comments, creating the conditions for crypto's strong rally even as equities remained under pressure. Brent eased slightly to $95.23 (▼0.31%) and WTI fell to $90.49 (▼0.34%) — modest moves that suggest the oil market is waiting for the same September 10 CPI data that will determine whether the rate-hold scenario or the rate-hike scenario prevails. August CPI and FOMC on September 16 remain the two critical decision points on the calendar.

Moderna and Merck reported that their personalized mRNA cancer vaccine significantly reduced the rate of melanoma recurrence in a clinical trial — one of the most consequential results yet for the mRNA platform's application beyond infectious disease, using the same messenger-RNA technology that powered COVID vaccines to train the immune system against individual patients' tumor antigens. The result positions personalized mRNA cancer immunotherapy as a clinically validated approach and opens a development path for mRNA vaccines targeting other cancers with patient-specific antigen profiles.

Scientists also discovered that more than 500 species of vesper bats possess two entirely separate sets of antibody genes — an immune arrangement never previously found in any mammal — a finding that raises fundamental questions about the evolutionary origins of mammalian immunity and what advantages this dual-system architecture confers on bats, which are known to host numerous viruses without becoming ill. Separately, researchers identified that aging is partly fueled by immune cells losing their capacity to clear away damaged, inflammation-promoting cells — a mechanism that, if druggable, could slow the cellular accumulation that drives age-related disease. Tea drinking was associated with slightly higher hip bone density over 10 years in a study of older women.
