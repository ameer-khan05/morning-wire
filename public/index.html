By the Numbers
$1.25T
OpenAI’s post-money valuation following Saturday’s $11 billion funding round — the largest private capital raise in AI history and the highest valuation ever recorded for a private technology company. The round follows OpenAI’s $6.6 billion raise in October 2024 and its accelerating commercial momentum, with the company reporting annualized revenue run rates that justify the premium relative to peers. At $1.25 trillion, OpenAI is now valued above every publicly listed company except Apple, Nvidia, and Microsoft — and above Alphabet, Amazon, and Meta. The funding arrives one day after OpenAI released GPT-6 Cyber and committed to its Wyoming data center, confirming that capital deployment is accelerating, not plateauing.

The American Heart Association’s Scientific Sessions 2026 continued with Day 2 of the Resuscitation Science Symposium at McCormick Place in Chicago; scientists published chemically confirmed evidence of ancient blood proteins preserved inside 115-million-year-old dinosaur fossils — the oldest verified organic material of this type — overturning prior skepticism about soft tissue preservation at geological timescales; and researchers unveiled a gel that can regrow lost tooth enamel by harnessing calcium and phosphate minerals naturally found in saliva, a potential paradigm shift for dentistry that would eliminate the need for synthetic fillings in early-stage decay cases.

The enamel gel is the week’s most practically disruptive science finding: tooth enamel does not regenerate naturally, and existing restorative dentistry is irreversible. A saliva-mineral approach that regrows enamel in situ would change the first-line response to early cavities across the profession.
