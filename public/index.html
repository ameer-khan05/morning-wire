The whale accumulation of 47,000 BTC is the structural counter-signal to the ETF outflow data: large holders are buying the dip while regulated institutional flows continue to exit. These two pressures define the market’s direction before the December FOMC hike removes any near-term rate relief.

PAHO’s revocation of US measles elimination status is the most significant US public health classification change in years. The US achieved elimination in 2000; losing and regaining it requires a minimum of one more calendar year without transmission — a timeline that depends entirely on vaccination coverage improvement.
