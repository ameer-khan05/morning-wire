Ethereum developers cancelled the Glamsterdam Hoodi testnet upgrade after Chainsecurity identified 13 deployed contracts with vulnerable behavior — specifically related to how the proposed gas changes would handle failed transactions — leaving the upgrade without a new activation date; Bitcoin slid to $80,506 and Ethereum fell to $2,359 with ETH now down 9.3% for the week, as Vitalik Buterin published EIP-8288 proposing recursive STARK proof aggregation and Bitcoin Core merged a fast-disclosure policy for flaws easily rediscoverable via AI.

Glamsterdam’s cancellation is categorically different from a delay: where a delay sets a new date, a cancellation means a protocol-level flaw requires redesign before any testnet schedule can be proposed. The 13 identified contracts are deployed on mainnet; any upgrade that changes how their failed transactions are handled permanently could expose them to unexpected behavior, and the fix must demonstrably clear all 13 before a new testnet can proceed.

Moderna and Merck’s Phase III trial of intismeran — a personalized mRNA vaccine that targets each patient’s specific tumor mutations — cut the risk of melanoma recurrence or death by 42% compared with Keytruda alone across 1,130-plus patients with resected stage 2B-4 melanoma, the definitive trial result that positions the platform for regulatory submission; a separate two-year clinical trial found that fish oil omega-3 supplements successfully delivered omega-3s to the brain but failed to improve memory or protect against Alzheimer’s-related brain shrinkage; and tracking data from 13 astronauts finds the human heart adapts well to spaceflight with regular exercise, with implications for Mars mission planning.

The intismeran result is the week’s most consequential single clinical outcome: moving from earlier-stage data to a Phase III trial with 1,130-plus patients produces the statistical power that regulatory agencies require for approval submissions, and a 42% risk reduction against a comparison arm that already includes Keytruda — a proven immunotherapy — is a result that demonstrates additive benefit over standard of care. The omega-3 trial’s failure is notable for its mechanism specificity: supplements reached the brain, ruling out the absorption explanation, which means the null result is a genuine pharmacological finding rather than a delivery problem.
