A once-nightly pill reduced sleep apnea breathing interruptions by 44% in a major phase 3 clinical trial, also improving blood oxygen levels and overall disease severity — one of the most significant results in sleep medicine in years for a condition affecting an estimated 936 million people worldwide. Alzheimer's researchers separately found that brain changes associated with the disease may begin at least 7 years before amyloid plaques become detectable with current PET scans — extending the theoretical early-intervention window by years and opening new targets for trials in presymptomatic patients.

A study in the Journal of Translational Medicine found that long Covid, PTSD, rheumatoid arthritis, ME-CFS, and multiple sclerosis — five conditions with distinct triggers — disrupt similar underlying biological systems, a cross-disease finding that could open shared therapeutic pathways. Separately, higher vitamin B12 levels in both mothers and fathers were associated with a lower prevalence of birth defects in their children in a study in the Annals of Internal Medicine — extending prior maternal-B12 research to include the paternal contribution to prenatal nutrition outcomes.
