By the Numbers
1,000,000
Maximum number of AI-equipped orbital data center satellites the FCC has authorized SpaceX to deploy in the Starmind constellation — the first full regulatory approval of an AI satellite fleet in US history, granted November 25. SpaceX must launch its first satellite by 2033. The American Astronomical Society petitioned to block the approval and was denied, with the FCC deferring light-pollution concerns to a later proceeding. Amazon’s Kuiper and Viasat also opposed. If the full constellation is deployed by the 2040 deadline, it would be the largest single orbital infrastructure project ever authorized by the FCC, adding more objects to low-Earth orbit than all prior satellite deployments combined.

The mRNA nose-drop result is a delivery-route advance: once intranasal mRNA is validated to reach brain cells at therapeutic concentration, the specific payload — what protein the mRNA instructs cells to produce — can be changed. Alzheimer’s and brain aging are the first use cases; Parkinson’s and other neurodegenerative conditions represent the broader research agenda the delivery route has just unlocked.
