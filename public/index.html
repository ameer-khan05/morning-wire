Princeton's Plasma Physics Laboratory published results from PACMAN — an AI framework that issues plasma control commands in 20 milliseconds and predicted a damaging fusion instability 200 milliseconds before it appeared across five real-world experiments, successfully preventing its formation — the sharpest experimental validation yet of AI-managed fusion control in a live reactor environment. At UC San Diego, researchers demonstrated for the first time that a cellular enzyme can accurately read an eight-letter genetic alphabet, doubling the four-letter code (A, T, C, G) used by all known life on Earth, opening new foundations for synthetic biology and information storage at the molecular scale.

Glioblastoma researchers found that blocking a protein called SET prevented tumor formation in preclinical models, while targeting related proteins made cancer cells more vulnerable to treatment — a potential new approach to one of the most lethal and treatment-resistant brain cancers. New guidelines on the heart disease–chronic kidney disease comorbidity pair found that simple early screening and treatment could substantially reduce the risk each condition accelerates in the other, addressing a high-prevalence, high-mortality clinical gap that has lacked clear management protocols.
