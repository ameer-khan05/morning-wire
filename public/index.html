Three new science findings arrived this week alongside the eclipse countdown: research in PNAS found that low arginine levels allow cancer cells and viruses to slip past immune surveillance by reducing production of a key cellular warning protein — a mechanism explaining why tumors and chronic viral infections can persist in healthy hosts. Scientists separately demonstrated a hydrogen turbine that generates electricity through detonation waves without a mechanical compressor, potentially removing the most expensive component from hydrogen power generation.

A long-term brain study confirmed four common cardiovascular risks — smoking, high blood pressure, heart disease, and high blood lipids — are strongly linked to the vascular brain damage that underlies vascular dementia, the second most common form of dementia after Alzheimer's. The finding reinforces aggressive cardiovascular risk management as a dementia-prevention strategy beyond the cardiovascular arguments alone.
