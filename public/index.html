August 12 will deliver one of the year's most remarkable celestial coincidences: a total solar eclipse and the Perseid meteor shower peak on the same day, with the New Moon that produces the eclipse also creating the dark-sky conditions that make the Perseids most spectacular — NASA projects 80 to 100 meteors per hour under ideal conditions, the finest display of 2026; the eclipse path crosses Russia, Greenland, Iceland, and Spain, making it the first total solar eclipse visible from mainland Europe since 1999, with partial coverage reaching North Africa, northern Europe, and parts of North America. Scientists separately identified the mechanism by which "jumping genes" — transposable elements that compose much of most genomes — move between species and accelerate evolution, a finding with broad implications for understanding how genetic complexity propagates across lineage boundaries.

A comprehensive review of more than 350 studies found that reducing dietary protein appears to lower inflammation markers and improve metabolic efficiency — a counterintuitive finding that challenges high-protein dietary orthodoxy and suggests moderate protein restriction may carry broad metabolic benefits beyond simple caloric reduction. Paleontologists working with Tyrannosaurus rex fossils from Wyoming published new findings on T. rex feeding behavior, providing fresh data on the mechanics and ecology of the Cretaceous apex predator that continues to yield biological surprises.
