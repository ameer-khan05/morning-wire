Intelligence reporting published Thursday revealed that Russia secretly assisted Iran in developing the C430L, an advanced supersonic cruise missile, through a multiyear program beginning in 2023 in which Russian missile specialists worked alongside Iranian engineers to develop ramjet propulsion technology — a disclosure that adds a direct Russian technological dimension to the missile and drone systems Iran has deployed during the Hormuz war. The revelation changes the strategic context of the US-Iran confrontation: the weapons capability Iran has deployed against US forces and regional targets was built with Russian engineering support.

The president's public characterization of Wednesday's airstrikes as "short-lived" in scope provided the diplomatic signal that eased Thursday's market risk-off, though oil prices held near $95 Brent, suggesting the energy market views the "short-lived" framing with scepticism while equities reacted more positively. Guinea-Bissau approved a new constitution Thursday with 70% of voters backing the charter — nearly 60% voter turnout — granting the president significantly expanded authority in what preliminary results showed was a decisive margin. Pakistan continues its Islamabad Talks process mediating the Iran war ceasefire track. Sweden holds elections September 13.

NASA's Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope is approaching launch with astronomers preparing extensive first-observation programs to exploit its wide-field infrared survey capability — designed to outimage Hubble in survey speed by a factor of roughly 200 and providing a survey-science complement to the James Webb Space Telescope for studies of dark energy, dark matter, exoplanets, and galactic structure at scales previously unreachable. Separately, avalanches like those that have devastated parts of Nepal and Tibet are becoming more common as climate change destabilizes mountain snowpack and permafrost, researchers report.

Researchers found that mindfulness, gratitude, and optimism training can measurably improve blood pressure, inflammation, and other heart-health measures within 8 to 12 weeks — a short enough timeline to be clinically relevant for near-term cardiovascular risk reduction. Scientists also determined that snake embryos begin life curled in a distinctive right-handed spiral, solving a developmental biology puzzle, and separately discovered how two famously poisonous plants synthesize chemicals with surprising medical potential — opening pathways for pharmaceutical applications.
