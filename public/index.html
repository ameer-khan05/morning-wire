Goldman Sachs agreed Wednesday to acquire NEOS Investments for up to $2.25 billion — landing the $1 billion BTCI Bitcoin High Income ETF (which generates 27% yield by writing covered calls on Bitcoin futures ETFs and spot Bitcoin ETPs), the NEOS Ethereum High Income ETF, and 17 other options-based ETFs spanning more than $30 billion in total assets. GLP-1 Oral Semaglutide Cuts Alcohol Cravings — CU Anschutz Trial: Fewer Heavy Drinking Days, Less Consumed, Weaker Cravings · Dopamine Dampening in Addiction Brain Circuitry · No FDA AUD Approval Since 2006 · Eclipse WB-57 Data Now in Processing · DBS Brain Network for Parkinson's Identified GLP-1 GLP-1 oral semaglutide (CU Anschutz): fewer heavy drinking days · less consumed · weaker cravings · mechanism: GLP-1 receptors in reward/addiction brain circuitry → dopamine dampening → addictive substances less rewarding · participants also: less marijuana use · no FDA AUD treatment since 2006 · eclipse WB-57: data in processing phase · DBS brain network for Parkinson's: identified (Aug 12)

The University of Colorado Anschutz Medical Campus published findings Wednesday that oral semaglutide — the active ingredient in Ozempic and Wegovy — significantly reduced heavy drinking days, lowered the amount consumed when drinking occurred, and weakened alcohol cravings in adults with alcohol use disorder, extending GLP-1's pharmacological footprint from metabolic conditions into addiction medicine. The mechanism is direct: GLP-1 receptors are active in the brain regions governing reward, motivation, and stress — the same circuitry hijacked by alcohol and other addictive substances — and at therapeutic doses, GLP-1 drugs cross the blood-brain barrier and dampen dopamine signaling, making addictive substances less intrinsically rewarding. Participants also reported decreased marijuana use, suggesting cross-addiction effects beyond alcohol.

Alcohol use disorder affects tens of millions of adults; the FDA has not approved a new AUD treatment since acamprosate in 2006. If oral semaglutide clears the regulatory pathway for an addiction indication, it would be the most consequential AUD pharmacology development in two decades from a drug already in widespread clinical use. NASA's WB-57 eclipse data from Wednesday's Spain airborne mission is now in initial processing; first peer-reviewed results comparing the solar-maximum corona to the 2024 minimum dataset are expected in 6-12 months. Separately, scientists reported identifying the specific brain network and electrical rhythm that drives deep brain stimulation benefits in Parkinson's disease — a finding that could enable precision targeting of DBS therapy.
