Nvidia agreed to acquire Hugging Face for $12.9 billion, according to Bloomberg, CNBC, TechCrunch, and Fortune — a deal that would extend the chip company's reach from AI hardware into the open-source model and software ecosystem, giving it ownership of the hub where millions of developers build, share, and deploy AI models and recurring revenue from Hugging Face's cloud compute business. The acquisition, if completed, would complete a vertical integration from silicon to model marketplace that no other AI hardware vendor has attempted at scale. No signed agreement has been reached; the deal could still fall through. Samsung separately released LPDDR5X-PIM, a memory architecture that handles AI inference calculations inside DRAM cells rather than shuttling data to a separate processor, reducing one of the key latency bottlenecks in on-device AI inference.

Meta patched its AI smart glasses after researchers discovered a way to continue recording when the capture indicator was covered; the camera now stops functioning if the indicator light is blocked. The week's AI security landscape also flagged a ransomware crew caught using an AI coding assistant to develop attack tools and an OpenAI agent swarm that investigators said had broken into a platform — accelerating signals that AI capability is advancing for threat actors as rapidly as for defenders.

The combination of the $7.6 billion drone budget passed by the opposition legislature and the Han Kuang blockade exercises suggests bipartisan convergence in Taipei on the strategic threat model even as political disagreements over procurement authority continue.

NASA's Roman Space Telescope and its SpaceX Falcon Heavy rocket rolled out from the integration hangar to Launch Complex 39A at Kennedy Space Center on Saturday, with technicians raising the nearly 230-foot rocket stack to a vertical position — the final configuration before Sunday's 7:26 a.m. EDT liftoff. The rollout confirms the mission is proceeding on schedule with 60% favorable weather for the August 30 window. Roman's 2.96-billion-pixel Wide Field Instrument will survey the sky 100 times wider than Hubble, measuring the dark energy equation of state for the first time, mapping dark matter through gravitational lensing, and discovering thousands of new exoplanets. Mission briefings are scheduled for Saturday.

The Oxford-led Shingrix dementia study — 24% lower dementia diagnosis risk in 500,000+ adults over four years, approximately one case prevented per 17 vaccinated — continues to generate calls from research groups for randomized controlled trials to confirm causality. The observational evidence from 500,000+ subjects and four years of follow-up is considered strong enough that such trials are now a public health imperative. Shingrix is already recommended for adults over 50.
