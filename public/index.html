The FDA PTSD clearance is Friday’s most consequential science development: it is the first time a regulatory agency has required individual neuroimaging as a component of a cleared psychiatric treatment protocol — institutionalizing precision-medicine logic in psychiatry rather than leaving it to research centers. The 68% remission rate in treatment-resistant PTSD, a population for whom prior interventions have failed, is the trial outcome; the precedent is that personalized brain mapping is now a required standard of care for a psychiatric indication, with downstream implications across TMS-adjacent psychiatric treatments.
