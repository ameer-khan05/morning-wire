By the Numbers
95%
Average task success rate achieved by Nvidia’s GR00T 2 — a 2-billion-parameter robot foundation model unveiled at the Conference on Robot Learning (CoRL) in Austin on Tuesday — after just five human demonstrations of a new task. The model was pretrained on 402,730 hours of first-person human video alongside 10,184 hours of robot teleoperation data across 2,897 tasks. GR00T 2 covers parallel-gripper arms, dexterous hands, and humanoid robots from a single pretrained checkpoint. Five-shot learning at 95% accuracy across 50 novel tasks is the benchmark that justifies calling this a robot foundation model: prior systems required task-specific training pipelines and far more demonstrations to reach comparable performance. Russia and Ukraine began the fourth round of US-brokered peace talks in Abu Dhabi on Tuesday with US envoys Witkoff and Kushner leading alongside Russia’s Vladimir Medinsky, as the agenda expanded beyond Ukrainian territorial disputes to encompass European security arrangements — a structural shift observers described as a concession to Russia’s core demand for a voice in post-war European security governance — with Ukraine continuing to reject any territorial concessions; Trump said he sees an opening for a peace deal and that the war “can end soon”; no ceasefire was announced on day 1; talks continue Wednesday in day 2.

The agenda expansion to European security is the most significant structural development in these talks. It signals that the conversation has moved from “stop shooting” to “redesign European security,” which is what Russia has demanded since at least 2021. Whether Ukraine and Europe accept that framing — or whether the expansion is a US concession made without European buy-in — is the question that determines whether day 2 produces a framework.

Beam Therapeutics announced its CRISPR base-editing therapy risto-cel met all primary and secondary endpoints in the pivotal BEACON trial across 49 sickle cell disease patients — sustained freedom from severe vaso-occlusive crises for at least 12 months — and plans an FDA BLA filing by year-end, which would make risto-cel the first base-editing therapy to receive FDA approval; the FDA formalized mandatory NAION labeling across all GLP-1 drugs including Ozempic, Wegovy, Zepbound, and Mounjaro after data confirmed the rare “eye stroke” risk more than doubles among two-year GLP-1 users versus other diabetes drug classes; and MIT scientists published research showing nanoparticles that reprogram immune cells inside the living body eliminated tumors and reversed liver damage in mouse experiments.

Beam’s BEACON pivotal success is not just a drug approval milestone — it is the first time a base-editing therapy has cleared a pivotal endpoint, establishing proof-of-concept for a new class of genetic medicine that corrects disease at the base-pair level rather than cutting DNA. The GLP-1 NAION warning is the week’s most broadly relevant health story, covering a drug class now used by tens of millions of patients globally.
