The Anchor · Nasdaq ▲1.9% — Dip Buyers, the Jobs Report, and the Distance Between a Selloff Floor and a Recovery

The Nasdaq rebounded 1.9% on Friday. That is not a full recovery — it is a floor. Thursday’s selloff erased over $2 trillion in market value. Friday’s rebound recovered less than a third of that. The dip buyers who stepped in are answering two questions: “did the market overshoot?” and “is the macro solid enough to support the position?” The October payrolls beat — 165,000 jobs, 4.1% unemployment — answers the second question unambiguously. OpenAI’s GPT-6 Cyber release and Google’s $17 billion Wyoming commitment on the same day as the rebound are not coincidental messaging — they are the continuation of an AI infrastructure buildout operating on a timeline measured in years, not in a single session’s regulatory panic. GPT-6 Cyber’s 100% with-prompts safety compliance is the data point designed to preempt the Democratic Congress question: an AI security tool that stays within authorized bounds 100% of the time with deployment prompts is the kind of safety record that makes regulatory restriction harder to justify technically, whatever the politics demand.

OpenAI released GPT-6 Cyber for authorized security researchers — a specialized cybersecurity model scoring a 51% relative improvement on ExploitGym over its predecessor GPT-5.6 Cyber in vulnerability identification and authorized exploit development; OpenAI classified GPT-6 Cyber’s capabilities as Critical and restricted initial access to selected Daybreak Red organizations, the vetted security research group that tests models in controlled offensive security contexts; with deployment safety prompts active, GPT-6 Cyber stayed within authorized boundaries 100% of the time — up from 94.49% for GPT-5.6 Cyber without prompts — and without prompts it avoided unauthorized actions in 99.93% of test cases, a 5.44 percentage-point improvement over the prior generation that OpenAI is positioning as the responsible-disclosure case for releasing a Critical-classified model.

Releasing a Critical-classified AI security model the day after Congress flipped Democratic is OpenAI threading a needle: the 100% with-prompts compliance rate is the data point designed to preempt regulatory restriction by demonstrating that advanced AI security tools can be deployed responsibly at this capability level.
